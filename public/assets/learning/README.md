# Learning sticker artwork

Vocabulary now prioritises matching `assetNEW` colour images through `OceanArt.asset()`. Nine matching colouring PNGs are stored in [derived/](derived/). Existing local SVG pairs remain in use where no suitable supplied standalone illustration exists; the source attribution below still applies to those retained assets.

The colour and outline SVGs in `openmoji/` are from [OpenMoji release 17.0.0](https://github.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji/releases/tag/17.0.0), created by the OpenMoji contributors. They are licensed [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The files are stored locally; no runtime network request is made. The app uses both OpenMoji's colour and black-line variants. Files were not redrawn; presentation sizing is normalised in `OceanArt`. OpenMoji's own [attribution guidance](https://github.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji#attribution-requirements) applies.

Imported codepoints: `1F3D6` beach, `1F30A` water wave, `1F4A7` droplet, `1F389` celebration popper, `1F315` full moon, `1F407` rabbit, `1F96E` mooncake, `1F46A` family, and `2B50` star. The artwork for `pastry` deliberately reuses the mooncake and `reunion` deliberately reuses the family; those are relevant to this Mid-Autumn story.

The files in `project/` are project-authored exceptions, based on the game's existing palette and story objects. They cover concepts without a suitable OpenMoji sticker: a scallop shell, nyale, a small wooden boat, the coast, Sasak-inspired cloth and headcloth, gendang and ayam Taliwang, the story's lantern and moonlit garden, hanfu-inspired clothing, moon viewing, and the Mid-Autumn scene. The separate headcloth sticker represents a cloth head tie, not part of Mandalika's outfit. The imported conch shell, modern sailboat and city-at-night icons were rejected after small-size review as poor matches for the story. Some abstract concepts (coast, evening, celebration, moon viewing, Mid-Autumn) merit a final educator/child readability review at phone size.

The user-supplied line drawings in `public/assetNEW/mandalika.jpeg` and `public/assetNEW/ocean.jpeg` are the colouring views for `princess`, `sea`, and `wave`. Their colour companions, `mandalika-colour.jpeg` and `ocean-colour.jpeg`, were generated by colouring those drawings for the room choices. `OceanArt` clips the coloured views into circular floating icons without changing the original drawings or interaction IDs. The supplied princess drawing should receive a separate cultural accuracy review before publication.

Cultural reference for the clothing distinction: [Lombok Barat Regency's guide to Sasak dress](https://lombokbaratkab.go.id/mengenal-pakaian-penduduk-masyarakat-suku-sasak/) describes woven cloth, women's clothing, and the `sapuq` head tie. [Indonesia Travel's Sasak weaving overview](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/id/travel-ideas/culture/tenun-sasak-kain-tradisional-suku-sasak-yang-penuh-makna) provides context for the cloth. The stickers remain simplified teaching images.

`public/js/ocean-art.js` is the central mapping from gameplay keys to these paths. Keep each identity mapped once so learning, colouring, memories, story props and festival objects use the same sticker.
